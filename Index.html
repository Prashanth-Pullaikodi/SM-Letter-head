ℹ️ How it works

A quick guide to a perfect letter.

1. Pick a template
Each template maps to a branded letterhead designed for a specific purpose.

2. Add recipient details
These replace the {RECIPIENT_DATA} tag in the letterhead.

3. Write & style your content
Rich text — bold, color, fonts and lists — is carried into the {LETTER_BODY} section of the PDF.

4. Generate
We build the document on the server, export a clean PDF, and start your download automatically as Generated_Letterhead.pdf.
